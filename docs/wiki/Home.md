## 🎯 Project Philosophy

Unlike traditional, rigid scrapers:
1. **Source Agnostic**: Works across LinkedIn, Indeed, Google Jobs, Glassdoor, Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, RemoteOK, Remotive, and Himalayas.
2. **Dynamic Role Modeling**: Not hard-coded to any single title. Expands seniorities and filters title collisions automatically.
3. **Consolidated High-Signal Deliverables**: Exactly one deduplicated, timestamped CSV output per session.
